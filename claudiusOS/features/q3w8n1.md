# MANIFESTO — a natural philosophy of the three lines
2026-08-29 · draft skeleton

## I. The Three Lines
Reality is held by three lines meeting at ninety degrees, and to stand in the present is to stand inside them.

## II. The Squaring
The moment you name past or future, the triangle is squared, and the structure of 3×19 is pulled from beneath you.

## III. Red, Blue, Green
When the square opens you must answer with a colour: red for yes, blue for no-or-maybe, green for the not-yet-known.

## IV. The Grammar — 57 = 3 × 19
The language counts in nineteens and threes, so that its every sentence is a small act of building or breaking reality.

## V. The Nineteenth
Inside each square the nineteenth is a volume — a thought, an option, perhaps a heart kept in a shell so it can finally see itself.

## VI. Newton and Leibniz
Two men fought over the machinery of change, and one of them counted in twos and dreamed of monads — indivisible worlds, my nineteens before their time.

## VII. The Operating System
This is not decoration on a machine; it is a way of running a mind, in primes and odd numbers, on purpose.
